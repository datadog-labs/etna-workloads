Compiles a lib with an E0308 mismatch between two very long associated types, with a tiny --diagnostic-width and -Zwrite-long-types-to-disk=no. The injected compiler (which lacks the textual shortening of highlighted strings and has a wider length threshold) fails to shorten the expected/found notes, printing the full multi-hundred-character types, whereas the clean compiler prints the shortened `<<<<_ as FooBar>::Wrapper<_> as FooBar>::...` form.
